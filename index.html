Frequently asked questions

Common compliance questions

Can Immanica manage monthly PF and ESI filings?

Yes. Support can include employee master review, contribution inputs, ECR or monthly filing preparation, payment coordination and filing records.

Do you support registrations and renewals across states?

Yes. Scope can cover state-specific PT, LWF, Shops & Establishments and other applicable registrations, subject to establishment details and local rules.

What information is needed to begin?

Typically, establishment documents, prior registration certificates, employee master data, payroll records, previous returns and details of the issue to be corrected.

Can you assess whether EEC is relevant to us?

Yes. An initial review can identify potentially eligible employee records and the documentation needed before proceeding.
