EEC 2026 — time-bound EPFO support

Regularise eligible left-out employees before the EEC 2026 window closes

The Employees’ Enrolment Campaign 2026 is open from 1 July 2026 to 31 October 2026 and covers eligible employees left out of EPF coverage during 1 April 2009 to 31 March 2026, subject to the campaign conditions. Immanica supports the operational process from eligibility review through digital declaration and payment.

Campaign deadline: 31 October 2026. Early record review is recommended so UAN, Face Authentication, ECR/TRRN and payment dependencies can be completed in time.
